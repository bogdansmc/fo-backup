fo-backup

fo-backup is a private, single-user backup tool. It lets its owner make a read-only copy of their own Google Drive to their own private backup storage.

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It only requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly). It never changes or deletes anything in Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
